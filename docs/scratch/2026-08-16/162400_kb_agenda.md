# KB agenda (not Synth gestures)

Synth agenda is a Deluge procedure→steps→items FSM with display
probes and HID gestures (`synth-cli agenda probe|confirm`). Early,
device-scoped.

Gaius `/agenda` is KB-oriented: `current/agenda.md` plus
`current/projects/**/agenda.md`. `ThetaAgenda` list|init. Sitrep
again suggests `/agenda init`.
